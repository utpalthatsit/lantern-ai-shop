What it does

Everything admin. Nothing busywork.

Six tools, one conversation. No dashboards to learn, no manuals — you just talk to it.

Answers customers, 24/7

Hours, prices, stock, directions — answered instantly on WhatsApp, Instagram DM, SMS or your web widget, in the customer's own language.

Bookings that don't ghost

Takes appointments, sends reminders, catches no-shows and rebooks them — so Marcus stops chasing empty chairs.

Inventory that talks back

"Sold 3 red shirts, size M." That's the whole update. Lantern tracks stock, flags low levels, and tells you what's flying.

Insights in plain language

No spreadsheets. Every morning: "Your best day was Saturday; iced coffee outsold everything 3-to-1."

Marketing drafts itself

Captions, WhatsApp broadcasts, promos for slow Tuesdays — drafted, ready for your one-tap approval. Never sent without you.

Multilingual by default

Hinglish, Tamil, Spanish, Arabic — your customers write in their language, Lantern answers in it. It's core, not a feature.
